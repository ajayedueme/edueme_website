Home Courses Augmented Reality / virtual reality

What you will learn:

  • Youngsters will learn how to create and program immersive digital experiences that can be used in various industries, such as gaming, education, healthcare, and entertainment.
  • They will learn how to design and develop 3D models, integrate audio and visual effects, and code interactive experiences using platforms such as Unity, Vuforia, and ARCore.
  • They will also learn about the differences between AR and VR, their applications, and how to create experiences that can be viewed on a variety of devices, such as smartphones and VR headsets.

Course Overview

This course teaches youngsters about Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) technology, including its uses and differences. Students will also use popular software tools to develop AR and VR experiences and explore the potential of these technologies in gaming, education, and healthcare. They will also learn how to ethically use AR and VR technology and its ethical and societal implications.

Why Augmented Reality / virtual reality?

Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality are rapidly growing technologies and have a lot of potential for various industries in the future. By introducing these technologies to kids at an early age, they can develop a fundamental understanding of how these technologies work and the various possibilities that they offer. Additionally, learning about these technologies can also foster creativity and imagination in kids, as they can design and create their own AR/VR experiences. It can also enhance their learning experience as they can explore and visualize complex concepts in a more interactive and engaging manner

Course Highlights:

  • Beginner Friendly
  • High Quality Training
  • Project development skill
  • Gain excellent technical skills

Who this course is for:

AR and VR can enhance learning for 3rd–10th graders. AR overlays digital visuals on the actual world, whereas VR creates an immersive virtual reality. These technologies can be used to recreate historical events, explore the human body in 3D, or practise coding in a pleasant and engaging way. AR and VR make learning fun and effective for kids. AR and VR will change how 3rd–10th graders see the world.
