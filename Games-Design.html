Home Courses Games Design (Android/iOS)

What you will learn:

  • You will learn a wide range of skills related to creating video games. These may include programming, graphic design, storytelling, and project management.
  • You will learn how to use different software tools and programming languages to bring your ideas to life and create interactive gaming experiences.
  • You will also learn about game mechanics, user experience, and game design principles, which are critical to developing engaging and fun games.
  • You will develop important skills such as problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration, which are valuable in many areas of life.
  • You will have the ability to create your own video games and a solid understanding of the game design process.

Course Overview

TKids will learn to design and build video games in a game design class. They will study game mechanics, user experience, and game design principles through hands-on activities and digital tools to make interesting and exciting games. Game creation requires programming, visual design, storytelling, and project management. Problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, and cooperation will help them improve personally and academically. Kids will learn how to develop games and construct their own by the conclusion of the session. Gamers, techies, and creative kids will love this course.

Why Games Design?

Learning game designing at an early age can be beneficial for kids in many ways. Firstly, it can help develop their problem-solving, critical thinking, creativity, and collaboration skills, which are essential for success in many areas of life. Additionally, gaming is a popular and growing industry, and by learning game design at a young age, kids can set themselves up for potential future career opportunities. Moreover, creating their own video games can help boost their confidence and self-esteem, and it can be a fun and engaging way to introduce them to technology and digital arts. Finally, learning game design can help kids understand the digital world they are growing up in and make informed decisions about the games they play and the technology they use. Overall, learning game design in early age is a valuable experience that can help kids develop important skills and prepare them for their future.
