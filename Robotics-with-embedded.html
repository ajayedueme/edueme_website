Home Courses Robotics with embedded C++

What you will learn:

  • Explain the operation and implementation of sensors, Arduino, and Dc motors and servo motors.
  • Describe different ways the sensors could be used.
  • Develop numerous projects using Arduino programming and excellent embedded system skills.
  • Look at the Arduino code used in projects and figure out what it does.

Course Overview

Younger generations will be inspired to learn more about robotics and its applications by this course. In addition, this training seeks to develop young innovators.

Why robotics?

The industrial and cultural revolution we are currently in is fueled in large part by robotics, which will have a significant impact on economics in the near future. 90% of employment in the modern economy require some level of digital skill, according to the digital economy and society index. They are needed for more than just computer and coding occupations. More workers with creative and problem solving abilities will be required as robots and artificial intelligence replace "simple" tasks. However, there must be more to the implementation of robots in classrooms than just financial ones.

Course Highlights:

  • Beginner Friendly
  • High Quality Training
  • Project development skill
  • Gain excellent technical skills (Electronics Knowledge, Programming Skills, Circuit Connection.)
  • Training Certificate

Who this course is for:

Our robotics education curriculum for 3rd–10th graders is enjoyable and helps children master skills in science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics (STEAM). Hands-on learning, personalised curriculum, and accessible delivery foster creativity, problem-solving, critical thinking, and innovation. Robotics education may give children a unique and useful learning experience that will help them in their future studies and occupations, and we are delighted to share further information about our programme and curriculum upon request.
