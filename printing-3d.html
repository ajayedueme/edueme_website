Home Courses 3D printing

What you will learn:

  • Design thinking: 3D printing allows youngsters to explore their creativity and design thinking skills. They can use digital tools to create 3D models and then print them into tangible objects.
  • Digital literacy: 3D printing requires the use of digital tools, such as computer-aided design (CAD) software. Youngsters can learn digital literacy skills by using these tools.
  • Problem-solving: 3D printing involves a lot of trial and error. Youngsters can learn how to identify and solve problems, such as improving the design or adjusting the printer settings.
  • Manufacturing processes: 3D printing is a form of additive manufacturing. Youngsters can learn about the different types of manufacturing processes and how they are used in industry.

Course Overview

Kids who want to learn about 3D printing can take this course. They'll design and print 3D models and troubleshoot 3D printers using hands-on activities and digital tools. Problem-solving, design thinking, and digital literacy will also be learned. By the end of the course, they will comprehend STEAM fundamentals behind 3D printing and how it can be used in many industries. 

Why 3D Printing?

3D printing lets kids practise their creative, problem-solving, and design thinking skills. CAD software helps them learn digital literacy. 3D printing is a fast-growing business that can prepare kids for employment. 3D printing is a fun way for kids to learn about technology and build valuable skills.

Course Highlights:

  • Beginner Friendly
  • High Quality Training
  • Project development skill
  • Gain excellent technical skills

Who this course is for:

3D printing is a fascinating technology that is suitable for students in 3rd to 10th grade. It allows youngsters to turn their ideas and designs into tangible objects through the use of digital tools and 3D printers. By learning about 3D printing, students can develop valuable skills, such as design thinking, problem-solving, and digital literacy.
